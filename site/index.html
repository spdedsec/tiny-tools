Useful tools. Nothing else.

Tiny Tools is a native Android utility kit for everyday calculations, conversions, dates, text, random choices, QR generation, and color inspection. It is deliberately offline-first, local-only, and free of accounts, ads, telemetry, and AI.

Download Android APK View source
Calculate

Arithmetic, percentages, tips, and bill splits with clear results and no hidden rounding.

Convert

Length, weight, temperature, and practical duration conversion kept simple and local.

Remember

Favorites and appearance preferences persist on-device through Room and DataStore.

Private by default.

Tiny Tools does not require an account and does not send your inputs to a server. The app works without a network connection.

Built for focus.

One tool at a time, restrained motion, readable type, and a dark charcoal interface with deliberate orange accents.
